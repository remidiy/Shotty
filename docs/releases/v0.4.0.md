**Watch your screenshot fly into the app.**

- Every capture now lifts off from where you took it and arcs into the window of the app it's going to. A soft ripple marks where it lands, while the paste happens at the same time (spec 0005).
- Full-screen captures shrink from the whole screen. Area captures start at their real size where you let go. "Send again" from History flies from the pointer.
- Respects **Reduce Motion**. Turn it off in Settings → Sending → *Animate captures into the app*.

Also includes everything from 0.3: sends to the app you were using, draggable history, ⌃⇧4 instant capture.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Download **Shotty.dmg** and drag Shotty to Applications, or use **Shotty.zip**.
